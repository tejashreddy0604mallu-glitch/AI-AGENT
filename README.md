# Historical AI Agent

> **Talk to the Greatest Minds in History using AI**

An AI-powered conversational platform that lets users interact with legendary historical personalities as if they were alive today. Search for a historical figure, select them, and begin an immersive conversation where the AI responds in the personality, knowledge, and speaking style of that person.

## Overview

Have you ever wondered...

-  What if Isaac Newton explained Gravity to you personally?
-  What if Nikola Tesla answered your questions about electricity?
-  What if Albert Einstein explained Relativity in simple words?
-  What if Leonardo da Vinci discussed his inventions with you?

This project makes that possible using AI.

Users simply search for a historical figure, choose them from the dashboard, and start chatting. The AI responds with knowledge limited to that person's lifetime while maintaining their personality, enthusiasm, communication style, and historical context.


##  Features

###  Smart Historical Search
- Search famous scientists, inventors, philosophers, mathematicians, leaders, and more.
- Easy-to-use dashboard.

###  AI Character Simulation
Each historical figure has:
- Unique personality
- Communication style
- Historical knowledge
- Emotional tone
- Period-accurate responses

###  Interactive Conversations
Ask questions like:
- Explain your discoveries.
- How did you make your invention?
- What challenges did you face?
- What inspired your work?
- What was life like during your time?

###  Time-Limited Knowledge
The AI only answers based on information available during that person's lifetime, making conversations more authentic.

###  Educational Experience
Perfect for:
- Students
- Teachers
- History enthusiasts
- Science learners
- Researchers

#  Example

### User

> Hi Newton, can you explain gravity?

### Isaac Newton

> Ah! An excellent question. While observing nature, I realised that the same force causing an apple to fall toward the Earth also governs the motion of the Moon around our planet. This universal attraction is what I call Gravity. Every object attracts every other object according to its mass and distance.

### User

> How did you invent calculus?

### Isaac Newton

> During the years when the plague closed the University of Cambridge, I devoted myself to mathematics. I developed methods to describe continuously changing quantities, which later became known as Calculus. It became one of my greatest mathematical achievements.
